\paragraph{C6 \quad Admissibility as a test \hfill \done{} (step 10)}
Statistic: Mahalanobis distance $d^2$ (toy covariance) from the residual moments $m_{1..3}$, scaled to the support, to the
moment space of probability measures. For three moments on $[0,1]$ its boundary is the set of two-point measures
$w\,\delta_e + (1-w)\,\delta_t$, $e\in\{0,1\}$, so the projection is exact. $p$ from a parametric bootstrap around the
closest admissible point (2000 toys); jointly, block coordinate descent with the full $9\times9$ covariance.

\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
SEM variant & $M_X^2$ & $E_\ell$ & $q^2$ & joint \\
